# Coffee Cinnamon — launch website summary

**Problem**
Cafés cook fixed amounts every day, so quiet days end with food in the bin.

**Approach**
Prepare closer to expected demand (a simple forecast from recent sales), and list whatever is still left for nearby
community kitchens and shelters to reserve and collect.

**Website experience**
- Customers: see what the café is, browse the menu, order for pickup, contact the café.
- Community partners: see today's surplus with quantity, pickup window and best-before, then reserve and confirm collection.
- Café staff: today's orders, tomorrow's prep forecast, surplus listings and messages in one dashboard.

**What we improved after testing**
- Homepage live numbers fixed at the root cause; one failing part can no longer blank the page.
- No more duplicate orders from double-clicks; stock limits and weekend-only offers are actually enforced.
- Expired food can no longer be offered to partners.
- The contact form now really saves messages; Contact and About are reachable on phones.
- Tablet layout, colour contrast, headings and keyboard focus fixed; demo figures are clearly labelled as sample data.

**Outcome**
A launch website that is more usable, accessible, honest about its data, and resilient when things go wrong,
tested at phone, tablet and desktop sizes.
